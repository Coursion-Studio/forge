Proof, not promises

Agents show the work passed before they say done.

A hand-back that claims “done” needs a passing test run on that exact code. Finished branches land on main through your own gate, and an install waits until every agent is idle, so no one gets cut off mid-task. App tests run in the background, not on your screen.

Questions

Who is Forge for?

Developers on a Mac who already work with coding agents like Claude Code and want several of them on real projects, with the terminal, browser, review and board right beside them. It is built for iOS, web and Mac projects.

Do I need an API key?

Not for Claude Code. Forge runs the Claude Code you already have, signed in with your own account. Keys are optional, for the built-in AI chat and model compare (Anthropic, OpenAI or a local model), and they stay in your Keychain.

Is it Mac only?

Yes. Forge is a native Mac app and needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.

When does it open?

We are inviting people from the waitlist in small groups. Join and you will get one email when your invite is ready.

What does it cost?

Free during the beta. Pricing will be announced before launch.
